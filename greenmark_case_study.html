Project Overview

Small business owners in India, such as café and restaurant operators, are eager to adopt sustainable practices. However, existing carbon markets are complex, enterprise-focused, and filled with technical jargon.

GreenMark bridges this gap by providing an intuitive, fintech-style app that simplifies footprint calculation, marketplace purchasing, and sustainability branding.

The Problem

Café owners lack awareness about carbon emissions and find existing carbon credit systems too expensive and complex. There are no simple tools tailored for Indian F&B businesses to measure and showcase their impact.

The Solution

An all-in-one mobile app offering a simplified 3-step carbon calculator, an e-commerce style marketplace for verified carbon offsets, and a shareable digital certificate to build customer trust.

Design System

The visual language blends "Minimalist Fintech" with an organic, eco-friendly touch to inspire trust and environmental action.
