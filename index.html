Pattachitra Art

Exploring the rich heritage of Pattachitra traditional art form with contemporary visual interpretation and design application.

✦ Animation / Dhingali
Research and Visual Development

Researching with an open mind and heart to find nuances, I've opted for the Banijara embroidery style, seeking guidance from the book "Textile of Banijara" for prime references. Delving into Law Garden Market, I aimed to grasp the intricacies of beauty in complexity for the background design. I'm actively exploring the visual nuances of Banijara embroideries, painting onto existing cloths adorned with Banijara's distinctive embroidery.

Heritage Origin
Derived from Sanskrit Patta (cloth) and Chitra (picture), this traditional art form is unique to eastern states of Odisha and West Bengal, each developing distinct identities with their own Geographical Indication (GI) tags.
Cultural Significance
Every stroke carries religious or social narrative. This sophisticated tradition reflects the rich cultural heritage and storytelling traditions of India's eastern regions, with narratives drawn from mythology and daily life.
Artistic Technique
Combining traditional embroidery techniques with visual exploration. The intricate patterns and designs showcase complexity and beauty in traditional textile arts, serving as inspiration for contemporary design applications.
Research Phase
Visual Exploration & Reference Study
• Textile pattern analysis
• Embroidery technique study
• Color palette development
• Visual narrative mapping
• Contemporary interpretation

Integrating traditional Pattachitra motifs into modern streetwear, bespoke footwear, and denim.
